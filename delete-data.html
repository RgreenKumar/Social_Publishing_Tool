User Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like PostFusion to delete your account and all associated Facebook and Instagram data, follow these steps.

  1. Email us at narenbala91@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Account.
  3. Include your registered email address.
  4. We will verify your request.
  5. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Deleted Information

  • Facebook User ID
  • Facebook Page ID
  • Instagram Business Account ID
  • Stored Access Tokens
  • Scheduled Posts
  • Connected Account Information

Once your request has been processed, your information cannot be recovered.
