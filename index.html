Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

Welcome to PostFusion. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how PostFusion collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your Facebook and Instagram Business accounts to our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Meta accounts, we may collect:

  • Facebook User ID
  • Facebook Page ID
  • Facebook Page Name
  • Instagram Business Account ID
  • Access Tokens provided through Meta OAuth
  • Basic profile information (name, profile picture)
  • Business account information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Connect your Facebook and Instagram accounts.
  • Publish posts to your Facebook Pages.
  • Publish posts to Instagram Business accounts.
  • Retrieve Page information.
  • Manage scheduled posts.
  • Improve platform functionality.
  • Provide customer support.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Access tokens are stored securely and are never sold or shared with third parties.

4. Data Sharing

PostFusion does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is shared only when required by law or when necessary to provide services through Meta Platforms.

5. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Disconnect your Facebook account.
  • Disconnect your Instagram account.
  • Request deletion of your stored information.
  • Request a copy of your stored information.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience, maintain login sessions, and enhance security.

7. Third-Party Services

PostFusion integrates with Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) using the Meta Graph API. Your use of these services is also governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes become effective immediately upon publication.
